1. Introduction

Taaskio Technologies India Private Limited (“Taaskio,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect personal information when you use the Taaskio mobile application (“App”), our website, and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

By downloading, installing, accessing, or using the Services, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, information generated through your use of the Services, and limited information from your device, as described below.

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account & profile: Name, mobile phone number, email (if provided), city/village, PIN code, language preferences, profile photo, skill categories, work area (rural/urban), experience, and optional notes about your work.
  • Identity & verification: Government ID details such as Aadhaar number and related verification data, OTP codes, and documents or images you upload for worker or customer verification (where permitted by law).
  • Bookings & communications: Service requests, job details, addresses or location descriptions, messages with support, ratings, reviews, and feedback.
  • Professional applications: Information submitted through “Join as Professional” forms on our website or App, including consent to verification and code of conduct.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device & app data: Device model, operating system version, unique device or advertising identifiers, App version, language, time zone, and crash or performance logs.
  • Usage data: Screens viewed, features used, referral source, and interactions with notifications.
  • Location data: With your permission, precise or approximate location to show nearby professionals, enable live job tracking, OTP-verified arrival, and route display on maps during active bookings. You can limit location access in device settings; some features may not work without it.
  • Log & security data: IP address, access timestamps, and anti-fraud signals.

2.3 Information from third parties

  • OTP and SMS/WhatsApp delivery providers for verification and service updates.
  • Identity or background verification partners (where you consent).
  • Payment or payout partners if in-app payments or settlements are enabled.
  • Map and analytics providers as described in Section 5.

3. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Create and manage your account and professional profile.
  • Match customers with verified workers and facilitate bookings.
  • Verify identity (including Aadhaar-based checks where applicable), OTPs, and Taaskio Assurance safety features.
  • Provide live job status, maps, and customer/worker notifications.
  • Process support requests and resolve disputes.
  • Improve, secure, and debug the App and website.
  • Send service-related messages (booking updates, verification, policy changes). Marketing messages are sent only where permitted by law and your preferences.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service and Worker Code of Conduct.

4. Legal basis (India)

We process personal data in accordance with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”), where it applies. Processing is based on your consent, performance of a contract with you, compliance with law, or legitimate uses permitted under applicable law (such as ensuring network and information security or preventing fraud), as relevant to each processing activity.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Other users: Limited profile and contact details needed to complete a booking (for example, first name, photo, skill, and job-related location during an active service).
  • Service providers: Cloud hosting, analytics, crash reporting, customer support tools, SMS/OTP gateways, mapping (e.g. Google Maps), and verification vendors under contracts that require appropriate safeguards.
  • Authorities: When required by law, court order, or to protect rights, safety, and security.
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, subject to notice where required by law.

Third-party services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them when you interact with linked services (for example, app stores or map providers).

6. App permissions (Android & iOS)

Depending on your role and features you use, the App may request permissions. Typical examples include:

  • Location — nearby search, navigation, and live tracking during jobs.
  • Camera / photos — profile pictures and verification document capture.
  • Phone / SMS — OTP auto-read where supported by your device and carrier.
  • Notifications — booking and safety alerts.
  • Storage — caching images or documents you choose to upload.

You may deny or revoke permissions in device settings; core features may be limited without them.

7. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide the Services, comply with law, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Verification data (such as Aadhaar-related records) is retained only for as long as necessary for verification, fraud prevention, and regulatory requirements, then deleted or anonymized where permitted.

8. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including encryption in transit, access controls, and secure handling of sensitive identifiers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; please use a strong device lock and notify us promptly of any suspected unauthorized access.

9. Children’s privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us at privacy@taaskio.com and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

10. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or update your personal information through in-app settings or by contacting us.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is consent-based (without affecting prior lawful processing).
  • Request erasure or restriction in certain circumstances.
  • Nominate another person to exercise rights on your behalf where permitted under the DPDP Act.
  • Lodge a grievance with our Data Protection Officer or contact us for escalation.

To exercise these rights, email privacy@taaskio.com with your registered mobile number and a description of your request. We may verify your identity before responding.

11. International transfers

We primarily process and store data in India. If we transfer data to service providers in other countries, we take steps consistent with applicable law to ensure appropriate protection.

12. Google Play & third-party store disclosures

This Privacy Policy is the same document linked from the Taaskio listing on Google Play and other app stores. The data types described in Sections 2–6 align with our Google Play Data safety form and in-app disclosures. If there is any conflict between a store summary and this Policy, this Policy controls.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version on this page and update the “Last updated” date. For material changes, we may notify you through the App, email, or SMS where required by law. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
