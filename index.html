person Meet the Founder

Building trusted work opportunities for every skill.

Kundarapu Kartheek founded Taaskio with a vision to bridge the gap between skilled workers and meaningful employment opportunities. His mission is to empower rural and urban communities by building a trusted, technology-driven workforce platform that connects people with opportunities through transparency, innovation, and reliability.

With an academic background in Agriculture for Sustainable Development from the University of Greenwich, UK, and experience in entrepreneurship and innovation across India and the United Kingdom, Kartheek is focused on solving real-world employment and workforce-access challenges through technology.

Through Taaskio, his goal is to create an inclusive ecosystem where skilled individuals—from rural workers and technicians to construction, household and professional service providers—can access better opportunities while households and businesses can find reliable, verified talent through one trusted platform.

Vision

To build the world's most trusted workforce ecosystem, creating opportunities, empowering communities, and transforming the future of work.

“Every skill deserves an opportunity. Every opportunity has the power to change a life.”

— Kundarapu Kartheek, Founder of Taaskio
